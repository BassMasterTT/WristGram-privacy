Privacy Policy

Your Privacy Matters

This Privacy Policy explains how CuffChat handles your data. We believe your data belongs to you — not to us, not to advertisers.

📅 May 2026

1. About This Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how CuffChat handles data when you use the app on Apple Watch. It covers data processed locally by CuffChat and clarifies when data is instead handled by Telegram or Apple as part of providing messaging, purchases, and platform services.

2. Data We Collect

CuffChat interacts with your Telegram account through Telegram's official API (TDLib). All message data flows through Telegram's infrastructure and is governed by Telegram's Privacy Policy.

Data accessed by CuffChat itself:

  • Messages & Chat Data — CuffChat displays message content (text, photos, voice notes, contacts, locations) on your Apple Watch. Message metadata (sender name, timestamp, chat title) may be temporarily cached locally on the watch.
  • Photos & Media — Photos you choose to send from CuffChat are transmitted directly to Telegram's servers. Photos received in Telegram chats are displayed on your Watch and may be temporarily cached locally.
  • Voice Messages — Voice messages you record and send are transmitted to Telegram's servers via your iPhone's network connection.
  • Contacts — When you share a contact from CuffChat, the contact information is sent to Telegram. CuffChat may access your Contacts with your permission to allow you to share contact details in Telegram chats.
  • Location — If you choose to share your location, CuffChat accesses your location data and sends it to Telegram.
  • Subscription & In-App Purchases — CuffChat uses Apple's In-App Purchase system (StoreKit) for premium subscriptions. Apple handles all payment processing. CuffChat does not collect or store your payment card information. Subscription status is stored locally on your device and in Apple's servers.
  • Device & Usage Data — CuffChat may temporarily log basic error and crash information for debugging purposes. CuffChat does not use analytics SDKs, third-party tracking tools, or advertising identifiers for behavioral profiling.
💡 What we don't collect
We do not use analytics SDKs, third-party tracking services, or behavioral profiling. CuffChat does not use your messages for advertising, profiling, or analytics, and message content is processed only as needed to provide the app's features.

3. How We Store Data

  • On-Device Storage — CuffChat stores minimal data locally on your Apple Watch (chat list cache, message metadata, preferences) using the watch's encrypted storage.
  • App Group Storage — Shared settings between the Watch app and any companion components use Apple's App Group container and the platform security protections provided by your devices and operating systems.
  • Telegram Servers — All messages, media, contacts, and shared content are stored on Telegram's servers. Please review Telegram's Privacy Policy for their data practices.
  • Apple Servers — Subscription and purchase records are managed by Apple per their Privacy Policy.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

CuffChat Local Data — Most locally stored CuffChat data is removed when you uninstall the app from your Apple Watch, subject to how watchOS manages app containers, backups, and system logs. You can clear locally cached CuffChat data by removing the app.

Telegram Data — All messages and media sent or received through CuffChat remain on Telegram's servers until deleted through the official Telegram app or telegram.org. To delete your Telegram account and all associated data, you must do so through Telegram's own account deletion process.

Apple Subscription Data — To cancel your CuffChat subscription, use the Subscriptions section in your Apple ID account settings (Settings → Your Name → Subscriptions) or visit apps.apple.com.

5. Third-Party Services

Telegram — CuffChat communicates exclusively with Telegram's servers through their official, documented API (TDLib). Your use of Telegram through CuffChat is subject to Telegram's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. We have no control over, and are not responsible for, Telegram's data collection practices.

Apple — CuffChat uses Apple services for app distribution, Apple Watch notification mirroring, and in-app purchases. Your use of Apple services is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

⚠️ Important
CuffChat is a third-party client and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Telegram F.Z. LLC. When you use CuffChat, your data is primarily governed by Telegram's own privacy practices, not CuffChat's.

6. Children's Privacy

CuffChat is not intended for children and is not knowingly offered to users who are below the minimum age required to use Telegram or the App Store in their jurisdiction. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make significant changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above and, if required by applicable law, notify you through the app. Continued use of CuffChat after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
