Privacy Policy

Your Privacy Matters

This Privacy Policy explains how WristGram handles your data. We believe your data belongs to you — not to us, not to advertisers.

📅 May 2026

1. Data We Collect

WristGram interacts with your Telegram account through Telegram's official API (TDLib). All message data flows through Telegram's infrastructure and is governed by Telegram's Privacy Policy.

Data accessed by WristGram itself:

  • Messages & Chat Data — WristGram displays message content (text, photos, voice notes, contacts, locations) on your Apple Watch. Message metadata (sender name, timestamp, chat title) may be temporarily cached locally on the watch.
  • Photos & Media — Photos you choose to send from WristGram are transmitted directly to Telegram's servers. Photos received in Telegram chats are displayed on your Watch and may be temporarily cached locally.
  • Voice Messages — Voice messages you record and send are transmitted to Telegram's servers via your iPhone's network connection.
  • Contacts — When you share a contact from WristGram, the contact information is sent to Telegram. WristGram may access your Contacts with your permission to allow you to share contact details in Telegram chats.
  • Location — If you choose to share your location, WristGram accesses your location data and sends it to Telegram.
  • Subscription & In-App Purchases — WristGram uses Apple's In-App Purchase system (StoreKit) for premium subscriptions. Apple handles all payment processing. WristGram does not collect or store your payment card information. Subscription status is stored locally on your device and in Apple's servers.
  • Device & Usage Data — WristGram may temporarily log basic error and crash information for debugging purposes. No personal usage analytics or tracking is collected or shared with third parties.
💡 What we don't collect
We do not use any analytics SDKs, third-party tracking services, or behavioral profiling. Your messages are never read, stored, or accessed by WristGram beyond what is needed to display them on your watch.

2. How We Store Data

  • On-Device Storage — WristGram stores minimal data locally on your Apple Watch (chat list cache, message metadata, preferences) using the watch's encrypted storage.
  • App Group Storage — Shared settings between the Watch app and any companion components use an encrypted App Group container shared with your other Apple devices.
  • Telegram Servers — All messages, media, contacts, and shared content are stored on Telegram's servers. Please review Telegram's Privacy Policy for their data practices.
  • Apple Servers — Subscription and purchase records are managed by Apple per their Privacy Policy.

3. Data Retention & Deletion

WristGram Local Data — Uninstalling WristGram removes all locally stored data from your Apple Watch. You can clear locally cached data at any time by uninstalling the app.

Telegram Data — All messages and media sent or received through WristGram remain on Telegram's servers until deleted through the official Telegram app or telegram.org. To delete your Telegram account and all associated data, you must do so through Telegram's own account deletion process.

Apple Subscription Data — To cancel your WristGram subscription, use the Subscriptions section in your Apple ID account settings (Settings → Your Name → Subscriptions) or visit apps.apple.com.

4. Third-Party Services

Telegram — WristGram communicates exclusively with Telegram's servers through their official, documented API (TDLib). Your use of Telegram through WristGram is subject to Telegram's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. We have no control over, and are not responsible for, Telegram's data collection practices.

Apple — WristGram uses Apple services for app distribution, Apple Watch notification mirroring, and in-app purchases. Your use of Apple services is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

⚠️ Important
WristGram is a third-party client and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Telegram F.Z. LLC. When you use WristGram, your data is primarily governed by Telegram's own privacy practices, not WristGram's.

5. Children's Privacy

WristGram is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

WristGram is an unofficial third-party Telegram client for Apple Watch:

  • A valid Telegram account is required to use WristGram.
